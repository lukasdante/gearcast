take one failing pump from first warning to approved repair. Target ~90 s (~220 words at a calm pace).

## 01 — Hook · Dashboard
> It's six in the morning at the Darong wastewater plant. Everything looks normal — except one pump.

## 02 — See it · Plant Overview + Inspector
> P one-oh-one B, the EQ transfer pump. Gearcast shows its vibration and bearing temperature climbing
> for three days straight. Not noise. A pattern.

## 03 — What's at stake · Shutdown cost + fault simulation
> If it trips, the equalization tank starts filling, and the plant downstream feels it. Gearcast puts a
> cost on that before it happens — and shows the standby pump ready to take over.

## 04 — Fix it · Troubleshoot
> The technician asks what's wrong. Gearcast answers from the plant's own manuals and this pump's
> repair history, step by step — with the parts needed, what's in stock, and where to find them.

## 05 — Parts · Spare Readiness + approval
> The bearing kit is on the shelf. A major spare goes to the supervisor for approval — one tap.

## 06 — Report it · Caster photo → incident
> From the field, the technician sends Caster a photo. Caster drafts the incident report on the plant's
> own form, photo included, ready as a PDF.

## 07 — Sign-off · Approvals
> The supervisor reviews the findings and approves. The maintenance record updates itself.

## 08 — Close · Dashboard green + end card
> One pump, one morning, caught early. Gearcast — maintenance that sees it coming.
