# GearCast MVP promo: narration script (draft v1, for validation)

Single narrator (Cartesia). About 400 words, about 2:40–2:50 at an upbeat, clear pace.
The on-screen captions use these same lines; the **bold** words are the emphasised caption words.

Cartesia notes
- Record one clip per scene: `00_hook.wav` … `13_close.wav`.
- Asset tags are spelled out for the voice ("P one-oh-one B"). On screen they stay as "P-101B".
- Leave about 0.4 s of silence at the end of each clip.
- Tone: energetic and confident. Scene 00 is a little darker and slower, then lifts from 01 onward.

Cast (role badges on screen)
- Lemuel: Plant Supervisor, PS-01
- Jun: Engineer, EN-01
- Mia: Technician, MT-01
- Ken: Technician, MT-02
- Rico: Technician, MT-03

---

## 00 — Hook · the problem (≈14 s)
> Six in the morning. Pump P one-oh-one A trips. The standby, P one-oh-one B, skipped its rotation six weeks
> ago. It doesn't start. Ten hours later, three production lines are down, and the bill is five point nine
> million pesos. All from one missed check.

Captions: **P-101A trips.** · Standby **fails to start.** · **10 hours.** · **₱5.9 M** lost. · One **missed check.**
*(Source: dataset loss scenario LS-10.)*

## 01 — Meet GearCast (≈10 s)
> That's the gap GearCast patches. One platform for the people who keep a plant's wastewater line alive: the
> supervisor, the engineer, and the technicians on the floor.

## 02 — Dashboard · Lemuel (≈10 s)
> Lemuel, the plant supervisor, starts the day on the dashboard. Every asset, every PM, every spare in one view,
> and one pump already flagged: P one-oh-one A.

## 03 — QR scan · Mia (≈11 s)
> On rounds, technician Mia scans its QR tag. Status, work orders, readings, past failures: the pump's whole
> history in one scan. And vibration is climbing.

## 04 — Plant overview + fault · Jun (≈14 s)
> Jun, the engineer, opens the 3D plant overview and asks, "what if it fails?" The pump turns red, the process
> line shows exactly which stage goes critical, and GearCast puts a price on the shutdown before it ever happens.

## 05 — Caster AI + troubleshoot · Mia (≈14 s)
> Back on the floor, Mia asks Caster, the GearCast assistant. Caster calls the plant's own tools: asset data,
> troubleshooting, spare stock. It answers with steps from the plant's manuals, the parts needed, and where
> they're stored.

## 06 — Spares + approval · Mia → Lemuel (≈11 s)
> Grease is an accessory, so Mia deploys it on the spot. The bearing kit is a major spare. That goes to Lemuel,
> approved in one tap.

## 07 — Incident report · Mia (≈15 s)
> Mia snaps a photo and tells Caster to write it up. Seconds later: an incident report on the plant's own form.
> The photo sits beside the summary, with a trend of every reading before the fault, from one week to one year,
> and the failure date marked.

## 08 — Repair or replace · Jun (≈12 s)
> Repair or replace? GearCast's XGBoost model weighs the pump's age, its failures, and the costs. And if it's
> replace, it names the brand, the supplier, and the lead time.

## 09 — Knowledge base · Jun (≈9 s)
> Need the procedure? Jun searches the knowledge base and opens the exact manual page the answer came from.

## 10 — Layout approval · Jun → Lemuel (≈9 s)
> Jun updates the pump area layout. Engineers propose, supervisors approve, and Lemuel signs it off.

## 11 — PM findings · Ken → Jun (≈13 s)
> The follow-up PM goes to Ken. On a tablet, Ken works the checklist, logs the readings, adds photos, and files.
> Jun approves, and the maintenance record updates itself.

## 12 — Emergency access · Lemuel → Rico (≈9 s)
> Plant two is short a technician tonight. Lemuel grants Rico temporary access, with an expiry, so it closes
> on its own.

## 13 — Close (≈12 s)
> One pump, caught early. Five point nine million pesos that stayed in the plant. GearCast: maintenance that
> sees it coming.

End card: **GearCast** · gearcast.engrlouis.dev
