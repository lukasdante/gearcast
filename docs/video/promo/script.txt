[01] Meet GearCast
That's the gap GearCast patches. One platform for the people who keep a plant's wastewater line alive: the supervisor, the engineer, and the technicians on the floor.

[02] Dashboard · Lemuel
Lemuel, the plant supervisor, starts the day on the dashboard. Every asset, every PM, every spare in one view, and one pump already flagged: P one-oh-one A.

[03] QR scan · Mia
On rounds, technician Mia scans its QR tag. Status, work orders, readings, past failures: the pump's whole history in one scan. And vibration is climbing.

[04] Plant overview + fault · Jun
Jun, the engineer, opens the 3D plant overview and asks, "what if it fails?" The pump turns red, the process line shows exactly which stage goes critical, and GearCast puts a price on the shutdown before it ever happens.

[05] Caster AI + troubleshoot · Mia
Back on the floor, Mia asks Caster, the GearCast assistant. Caster calls the plant's own tools: asset data, troubleshooting, spare stock. It answers with steps from the plant's manuals, the parts needed, and where they're stored.

[06] Spares + approval · Mia → Lemuel

[07] Incident report · Mia

[08] Repair or replace · Jun
Repair or replace? GearCast's XGBoost model weighs the pump's age, its failures, and the costs. And if it's replace, it names the brand, the supplier, and the lead time.
